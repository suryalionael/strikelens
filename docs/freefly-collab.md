# Freefly collaboration

StrikeLens is built for a proposed collaboration with Freefly Metrics
(@freefly.combat), an Indonesian media account that publishes hand-counted
kickboxing stats on Instagram. Nothing is agreed yet. This page holds the pitch,
the questions, and notes from every conversation.

## Pitch summary

**Count a fight in a fraction of the time, with numbers you can still trust.**

- Freefly keeps full control of every number. The tool suggests; the analyst decides.
- Hotkey tagging is faster than pausing and writing by hand, even before any CV.
- CV flags likely strikes so the analyst can jump between them instead of watching
  every second at slow speed.
- Stats and Instagram-size stat cards come out automatically.
- It is free and open source. Freefly does not pay for anything.
- What we ask for: feedback from people who count fights for real, their counting
  rules, and one already-counted fight to compare against.

## First DM outline (issue #4)

Keep it short. Indonesian or English, whichever Freefly posts in more.

1. Who I am: Data Science and Analytics student, web developer, and why their
   stats posts caught my attention.
2. What I am building, in one line, and that it is free and open source.
3. One ask: a 20-minute call, or answers to two questions (how they define a landed
   strike, and whether they would share one counted fight).
4. Link to this repo.

## Open questions

The full list lives in [stat-definitions.md](stat-definitions.md#open-questions-for-freefly).
The five to ask first:

1. How do you define a landed strike?
2. What counts as one attempt in a combination?
3. Which stats take you longest to count today?
4. Can you share one fight you have already counted, with your numbers?
5. Do you want the tool private to your team or public?

Collaboration questions:

- Who owns tags made in StrikeLens on Byon footage? Can any become a public dataset?
- Can StrikeLens be credited on cards that use it?
- Are you in contact with Byon Combat, and would they allow footage in demos?
- Who on your team would test the tagger, and how often do you count fights?
